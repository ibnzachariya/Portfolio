Oil & Gas Compliance Management Platform

A comprehensive desktop application developed to simplify equipment management, maintenance tracking, compliance monitoring and operational decision-making within the oil and gas industry.

Project Overview

This application was designed to improve operational efficiency by providing an integrated platform for managing oil and gas equipment, maintenance schedules, vendor information, regulatory news, compliance reports and weather advisories.

Key Features

Equipment Management

Register, update and monitor oil & gas equipment.

Maintenance Scheduling

Record maintenance history and monitor equipment health.

Vendor Comparison

Compare equipment prices from multiple vendors.

Regulatory News

Automatically retrieves the latest industry news and updates.

Weather Advisory

Displays weather conditions affecting field operations.

Analytics Dashboard

Visualize maintenance trends and equipment performance.

Technologies Used

Python Tkinter Pandas BeautifulSoup Selenium Matplotlib CSV

Challenges & Solutions

During development, I tackled several practical challenges, including managing persistent data with CSV files, integrating live regulatory news and weather information, automating web data retrieval, generating analytical charts, and packaging the application into a standalone Windows executable for distribution.
